# Final demonstration

1. Open the assigned HTTPS workspace URL. Show native and Google entry points
   and the registration approval requirement. For the review flow, use an
   authorized temporary validation identity and a separate administrator session.
2. Confirm the email, sign in, and show the pending state. Attempt the
   analytics page and an authenticated analytics API read. Show the denial.
3. Open **User administration** in the administrator session. Review the
   pending list and confirm approval. Show the resulting audit record. The
   applicant refreshes account status and enters the workspace.
4. Open Canada and inspect network coverage. Open Peru to show the unavailable
   network state. Explain that CVE exposure remains unavailable in both
   countries. Display source date and provenance for the Canadian context.
5. Open a CVE card from the relationship view. Record the snapshot and evidence
   identifier. Choose **How to mitigate** and show ordered progress and the
   live evidence-backed report. Open its source and show the editable handoff.
6. Demonstrate replay and cancellation from the authorized workspace. Suspend
   the temporary user, then check the analytics API with its prior token.
   Restore access and start a fresh sign-in. Show password recovery and logout
   only with a controlled validation mailbox. Disable temporary users after the demo.
7. Close with the measured grounding denominator, latency, tokens, Lakehouse
   parity and remaining acceptance items from the evaluation report.
